How to Make a Paper Organizer

Follow these steps to create a practical paper organizer for your desk.

1

Prepare Your Paper

Choose a sturdy piece of paper or cardstock (A4 or letter size works well). Decorative scrapbooking paper adds a nice touch.

2

Create the Base Folds

Fold the paper in half lengthwise, then unfold. Fold both sides to meet at the center crease. This creates four equal sections.

3

Form the Compartments

Turn the paper 90 degrees and repeat the folding process. You should now have a grid of 16 smaller rectangles.

4

Assemble the Organizer

Fold along the creases to create walls for your organizer. Use glue or double-sided tape to secure the corners in place.

5

Customize and Use

Decorate your organizer as desired. Once dry, place it on your desk and use the compartments to organize pens, paperclips, and other small items.
